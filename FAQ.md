# Mini Racer FAQ

Quick answers to common questions. For the full overview, see [README.md](README.md).

## Playing the game

### What is Mini Racer?

A free, one-lap racing game that runs inside Reddit posts. Open a Mini Racer post, race today’s track, and try to beat your best time.

### Where do I play?

In any subreddit that has Mini Racer installed. Open a Mini Racer post and tap **Race Now** (or **Race**).

### How do I steer?

- **Desktop:** A / D or left / right arrow keys
- **Phone:** On-screen left and right buttons
- **Restart:** Press **R**, or use the restart control on screen

You can also move through menus with the arrow keys or WASD, and press Enter to confirm.

### Does hitting a wall end my run?

No. By default you scrape the wall, lose speed, and keep going. If you want a bump to restart the attempt, turn on **Collision Auto-Restart** in Settings (optional; off by default). You can also set a short delay before the auto-restart.

### What is today’s track, and when does it change?

A new featured track appears each day at **midnight UTC** (not your local midnight). Once that day’s track is set, it does not get swapped for something else.

### Can I play older tracks?

Yes. Open **Tracks** to replay daily tracks from the last **7 days**. After that, they leave the list.

Your personal best and ghost for a track stay available until **6 hours** after that track drops off the 7-day list.

### Why does an old post open today’s track?

Older posts keep their original track while it is still available. If that day’s track has expired, the post sends you to **today’s** featured race instead.

### What are Standings?

The daily leaderboard for that day’s track. Times are ranked to the millisecond. Your own rank stays visible even if you are far down the list. On mobile you can swipe left/right to change days.

### What is the ghost?

A faint copy of your best verified lap on that track. It is on by default; turn it off in Settings → **Personal Best Ghost**. The ghost uses your current Garage look and does not collide with you. If it is not ready when the race starts, you race without it and see a short **GHOST UNAVAILABLE** notice.

### What are medals?

Target times on each track: bronze, silver, gold, and author. Beat them to earn the matching medal.

### Does the Garage change how the car drives?

No. Skins and trails are looks only. There are 12 skins (Mini, Cyberpunk, Steampunk) and 8 trails (including no trail).

### How do I share a time?

- After a finish: **Share Time**
- From Standings: **Share Best** (when you have a verified time for that day)

You preview the comment first, then it posts as **your** Reddit comment in the post’s pinned **score thread**. Sharing the same result again reuses that comment instead of posting a duplicate. If you delete the comment and share again, a new one is created.

### Will people see my Reddit username?

Only if **Username** is On in Settings. If it is Off, Standings and shares use a generated racer name (for example, something like “Arctic Falcon”).

### Why did my progress or settings reset?

Your car look and settings are saved to your player profile:

- **Guest** (not signed in): data expires after about **7** inactive days
- **Signed in** to Reddit: data expires after about **30** inactive days

If you play as a guest and lose that guest session, you may get a new guest identity (cosmetics saved on the device may still look the same).

### Is Mini Racer free? Are there purchases?

Yes, it is free. There are no in-game purchases.

### Why didn’t my time show on the leaderboard?

Times need to pass a server check (the game re-runs your lap to make sure it is valid). Suspicious or mismatched runs are rejected. You can try again before that day’s competition window ends.

## For subreddit moderators

### How do I add Mini Racer to my community?

Install the Mini Racer app in your subreddit, then use the subreddit menu:

| Menu item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Create Mini Racer post** | Creates a playable race post for today |
| **Enable daily Mini Racer posts** | Auto-posts a new race each day around **00:05 UTC** |
| **Disable daily Mini Racer posts** | Stops the daily race auto-posts |
| **Enable daily Mini Racer podium posts** | Auto-posts the final top 3 after a track leaves the 7-day window |
| **Disable daily Mini Racer podium posts** | Stops podium auto-posts only (race posts are unchanged) |

### Will this spam the subreddit feed?

No. There is only **one** race post per community per UTC day. Creating again reuses that post. Every race post also gets a pinned **score thread** for shared results.

### What are podium posts?

Optional, separate posts that show the final top three verified times when a track’s play window ends. They are informational, use their own on/off toggle, and do **not** create a score thread. **Play Now** on a podium post opens today’s featured track.

## Still stuck?

If something looks wrong in-game, check Settings first (sound, ghost, collision restart, username). For community setup, confirm Mini Racer is installed and that daily posts are enabled if you expect an automatic post each day.
